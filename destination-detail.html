Traditional Attire & Modesty

Honor holy ground by observing customary attire rules established by resident religious custodians.

  • ✦ South Asian Temples: Traditional unstitched dhotis/saris preferred for inner sanctum entry.
  • ✦ Gurdwaras & Mosques: Full head coverage required for all visitors; modest loose-fitting clothes.
  • ✦ Holy Land & Cathedrals: Knees and shoulders strictly covered; hats removed upon entry in churches.
🦶

Footwear Removal & Cloakrooms

Entering holy sanctums barefoot symbolizes humility, equality, and shedding external dust.

  • ✦ Dedicated Token Counters: Deposit footwear safely at verified temple footwear counters.
  • ✦ Foot Washing: Step through running water channels at Gurudwara entries to purify feet.
  • ✦ Heated Marble: Cotton walking socks permitted on sun-warmed stone corridors in summer.
🏺

Sacred Offerings & Holy Waters

Participate thoughtfully in time-honored rituals, receiving blessings and sanctified food.

  • ✦ Ganga Jal & Holy Water: Collect sanctified water in sealed copper or food-grade brass vessels.
  • ✦ Prasad Handling: Always receive sanctified offerings with both hands or right hand open.
  • ✦ Charity Desks: Channel all monetary donations directly through official trust receipt counters.
🤫

Silence & Inner Sanctum Rules

Preserve devotional intimacy and silence required for profound spiritual reflection.

  • ✦ Mobile Phones: Switch devices completely off or leave them in secure hotel lockers.
  • ✦ No Photography: Respect total camera bans in garbhagrihas, altars, and prayer zones.
  • ✦ Circumambulation: Follow traditional clockwise parikrama pathways around sanctums.
