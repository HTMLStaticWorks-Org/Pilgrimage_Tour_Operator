Sacred Etiquette: Visiting with honor & respect

Read stewardship principles →
👘

Attire & Modesty

  • Hindu Temples: Dhotis or saris; covered shoulders and knees.
  • Gurdwaras & Mosques: Head coverings mandatory; scarves provided.
  • Cathedrals: Modest coverage; no shorts or sleeveless tops.
📷

Photography Demarcations

  • Inner Sanctums: Photography strictly forbidden at altars.
  • Worshippers: Never photograph individuals during prayer.
  • Commercial Media: Advance custodial permits required.
🦶

Footwear & Purity Rituals

  • Barefoot Zones: Deposited safely at verified cloakrooms.
  • Ablutions: Foot-washing channels at Gurudwara entries.
  • Socks Policy: Cotton socks allowed on hot marble walkways.
🕊️

Prasad, Seva & Offerings

  • Langar (Sikhism): Free sanctified meals served to all equally.
  • Prasadam (Hinduism): Received reverently with the right hand.
  • Charity: Channeled only through official trust receipt counters.
