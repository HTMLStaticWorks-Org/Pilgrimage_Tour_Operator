CULTURAL REVERENCE

Sacred Etiquette: Visiting with honor & respect

Read our stewardship principles →
👘

Attire & Modesty

Each sacred sanctum observes traditional dress standards honoring the divine presence.

  • Hindu Temples: Dhotis/saris preferred; shoulders and knees covered.
  • Gurdwaras & Mosques: Head covering mandatory; clean scarves provided.
  • Cathedrals & Basilicas: Modest coverage; no sleeveless tops or shorts.
📷

Photography Demarcations

Preserve devotional intimacy and honor sacred site prohibitions with utmost care.

  • Inner Sanctums: Photography strictly forbidden in garbhagrihas and altars.
  • Worshippers: Never photograph individuals during intense prayer.
  • Commercial Media: Special written custodial permits required in advance.
🦶

Footwear & Purity Rituals

Entering holy ground begins with shedding worldly dust at dedicated safe cloakrooms.

  • Barefoot Zones: Removed at temple thresholds and Gurudwara sarovars.
  • Ablutions: Foot-washing water channels provided at Gurudwaras and Mosques.
  • Socks Policy: Non-slip cotton socks permitted on sun-heated marble walkways.
🕊️

Prasad, Seva & Offerings

Participate mindfully in communal meals, sacred offerings, and sanctum blessings.

  • Langar (Sikhism): Free sanctified meals served to all seated equally.
  • Prasadam (Hinduism): Receive with the right hand with reverence.
  • Charity: Give only via authorized temple trust donation receipt counters.
