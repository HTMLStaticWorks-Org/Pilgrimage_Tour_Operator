🌿

Gentle & Senior-Friendly

Designed for elderly pilgrims or travelers needing unhurried rest, step-free access, and zero exhaustion.

  • ✦ Maximum 2 hours driving time per day
  • ✦ Single-hotel or 2-hub stays to avoid frequent packing
  • ✦ Wheelchair ramps, elevator-equipped hotels, and palki bookings
  • ✦ Proximity to multi-specialty medical centers
🥾

Wayfarer & Walking Trail

For pilgrims desiring to experience sacred geography on foot, along ancient pathways and pilgrim stone trails.

  • ✦ 8 to 15 km of mindful walking per day
  • ✦ Luggage transported ahead to evening sanctuaries
  • ✦ Escort support vehicle on standby at trail junctions
  • ✦ Monastic lodge stays and traditional hot pilgrim meals
⚡

Comprehensive Multi-Tirtha

For pilgrims seeking to cover extensive sacred circuits (such as 12 Jyotirlingas or 5 Takhts) with energetic devotion.

  • ✦ Early dawn departures for auspicious mangala aartis
  • ✦ Express private transfers via expressways & Vande Bharat trains
  • ✦ Efficient pre-booked priority queue lines
  • ✦ Rich daily debriefs with dedicated cultural historians
